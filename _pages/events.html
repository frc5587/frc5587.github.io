Check out our upcoming events!

Icebreaker Competition September 26

Come join us at Icebreakers, where we will be performing last year's game, REBUILT, with team 614 at Hayfield Secondary School.

CRI Showcase September 27

Come see our team's robot, Bugbert, at the CRI Community Showcase, where we will be introducing our team and FIRST Robotics and giving people the a chance to try driving or robot.

Art on the Avenue October 3

Come see our booth at Art on the Avenue, an annual event along Mount Vernon Avenue in Del Ray. Come see us demonstrate our robot and try your hand at fun activities and even at driving our robot!

Spooky Science Expo October 17 (Rain day: 24)

Come to Spooky Science Expo, a Halloween-themed day of science! Our team will be there, doing fun STEM activities and demonstrating our robot.

MacArthur STEM Night November 10

Join us at MacArthur STEM Night, held at MacArthur Elementary School, where our robotics team will present an exciting robot demonstration, allowing students to drive it for themselves and share information about our team and programs. This event is a wonderful chance for students to explore STEM through interactive demos and activities!

Bunnybots December 12

Come see us at our main offseason competition, Bunnybots, hosted by team 449 at Montgomery Blair High School! It's a fun opportunity for new team members to experience the actual format of a FIRST robotics competition and a way to experience the competition before the actual season. It also offers a great way to get experience before the main season.
